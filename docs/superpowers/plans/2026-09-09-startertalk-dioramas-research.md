## paper: 1964

**Message.** The founding quark paper already lists the five-quark configuration; the slide shows Gell-Mann's sentence, the 3D world shows Zweig's title page.

**On screen.**

"Baryons can now be constructed from quarks by using the combinations (q q q), (q q q q q̄), etc., while mesons are made out of (q q̄), (q q q̄ q̄), etc."

— M. Gell-Mann, Phys. Lett. 8 (1964) 214

## neutrals: Where the neutrals go missing

**Message.** The Σc⁺D̄⁽*⁾⁰ pair whose thresholds the narrow Pc states sit under always decays with a π⁰ or γ; LHCb's open-charm observation used the all-charged isospin partner, so the exact-threshold channel needs partial reconstruction.

**On screen.**

Pc(4312)⁺ and Pc(4457)⁺ sit about 5 and 2 MeV below the Σc⁺D̄⁰ and Σc⁺D̄*⁰ thresholds.

Σc⁺ → Λc⁺π⁰.  D̄*⁰ → D̄⁰π⁰ or D̄⁰γ.

Every decay of the pair leaves a neutral.

LHCb, PRL 122 (2019) 222001; PDG 2024

**Notes.**

Budget 1.25 min.

0:00–0:20  Thresholds. LHCb's discovery paper quotes Pc(4312)⁺ and Pc(4457)⁺ "approximately 5 MeV and 2 MeV below the Σc⁺D̄⁰ and Σc⁺D̄*⁰ thresholds" and Pc(4440)⁺ "the second ΣcD̄* state, with about 20 MeV of binding energy"; it also notes the Σc⁺⁺D̄⁽*⁾⁻ thresholds lie about 5 MeV higher (arXiv:1904.03947, PRL 122 (2019) 222001; masses Table 1: 4311.9 ± 0.7 +6.8/−0.6, 4440.3 ± 1.3 +4.1/−4.7, 4457.3 ± 0.6 +4.1/−1.7 MeV). With PDG 2024 fit masses (Σc⁺ 2452.65, Σc⁺⁺ 2453.97, D⁰ 1864.84, D⁺ 1869.66, D*⁰ 2006.85, D*⁺ 2010.26 MeV): Σc⁺D̄⁰ 4317.5, Σc⁺⁺D⁻ 4323.6, Σc⁺D̄*⁰ 4459.5, Σc⁺⁺D*⁻ 4464.2 MeV. Gaps: Pc(4312)⁺ 5.6 below Σc⁺D̄⁰ and 11.7 below Σc⁺⁺D⁻; Pc(4457)⁺ 2.2 below Σc⁺D̄*⁰ and 6.9 below Σc⁺⁺D*⁻; Pc(4440)⁺ 19.2 and 23.9 MeV. Caveat if asked: the +6.8 and +4.1 MeV upward systematics exceed the gaps to the neutral thresholds, so "below" is not established at 1σ for Pc(4312)⁺ and Pc(4457)⁺.

0:20–0:40  Decay chains (PDG 2024). Σc(2455) → Λc⁺π is the only strong decay open (≈100 %); for Σc⁺ that is Λc⁺π⁰, always (p* = 93.6 MeV/c). D*(2007)⁰ → D⁰π⁰ (64.7 ± 0.9) % or D⁰γ (35.3 ± 0.9) %; D⁺π⁻ is closed, m(D*⁰) = 2006.85 < 2009.23 MeV (p*(π⁰) = 42.6 MeV/c, E*(γ) = 137 MeV). Neutral count per Λb⁰ → ΣcD̄⁽*⁾K⁻ charge channel, with Λc⁺ → pK⁻π⁺, D⁻ → K⁺π⁻π⁻, D̄⁰ → K⁺π⁻: Σc⁺⁺D⁻ zero; Σc⁺⁺D*⁻ zero in 67.7 % (D̄⁰π⁻), one in 32.3 % (D⁻π⁰ 30.7 % + D⁻γ 1.6 %); Σc⁺D̄⁰ one, always; Σc⁺D̄*⁰ two, always (the 0.4 % D̄⁰e⁺e⁻ mode aside). Same counts with Σc(2520). Note the D̄⁰ is a neutral particle too but reconstructible; say "no unreconstructed neutral", not "fully charged".

0:40–1:00  What LHCb has observed. "First observation of Λb⁰ → Σc⁽*⁾⁺⁺D⁽*⁾⁻K⁻ decays", arXiv:2404.19510, PRD 110 (2024) L031104, Run 2, 6 fb⁻¹: four modes Σc⁺⁺D⁻K⁻ (480 ± 25, 32σ), Σc*⁺⁺D⁻K⁻ (279 ± 26, 13σ), Σc⁺⁺D*⁻K⁻ (243 ± 17, 21σ), Σc*⁺⁺D*⁻K⁻ (116 ± 15, 9σ); B(Σc⁺⁺D⁻K⁻)/B(Λc⁺D̄⁰K⁻) = 0.282 ± 0.016 ± 0.016 ± 0.005. All final states are charged tracks. The paper states O(100) candidates per mode, too few for an amplitude analysis, Run 3 needed; it does not search the ΣcD̄ spectra for Pc states. The Σc⁺D̄⁽*⁾⁰K⁻ channel appears only as the bracketed alternative "[Λc⁺/Σc⁺]", "[D⁽*⁾⁰]" in its Fig. 1 (colour-suppressed diagram) — not reconstructed, no limit. So the open-charm test at the thresholds the PRL quotes is, so far, done in the isospin-partner channel. That last sentence is my reading, not the paper's.

1:00–1:20  Recovering the neutral. One missing particle of known mass (π⁰: 134.98 MeV; γ: 0) is three unknowns. The Λb⁰ flight direction from primary and decay vertex gives two equations; the Λb⁰ mass gives a third, quadratic in |p(Λb⁰)|, so two solutions. LHCb has used exactly this for a missing neutrino: |Vub| with Λb⁰ → pμν (arXiv:1504.01568, Nature Phys. 11 (2015) 743): q² "only up to a two-fold ambiguity", resolutions about 1 and 4 GeV², candidates with both roots imaginary rejected; Λb⁰ → Λc⁺μν shape (arXiv:1709.01920, PRD 96 (2017) 112005), Eq. 6, lower root correct 50–60 % of the time. An intermediate mass — m(Σc⁺) on Λc⁺π⁰ or m(D̄*⁰) on D̄⁰π⁰/γ — is a fourth equation for the same three unknowns: one overconstraint, which is what a decay-tree fit uses (Hulsbergen, NIM A 552 (2005) 566: missing particles allowed "provided the tree is not kinematically under-constrained"). The fourth-constraint step is generic kinematics, not from any published LHCb analysis; say "in general". Why the ECAL route is hard: analyses cut on photon pT (200 MeV/c in the performance paper's photon-ID study, 250 MeV/c in the B⁺ → J/ψK*⁺ π⁰ efficiency study); resolved π⁰ mass resolution 8 MeV/c².

1:20–1:25  The LHCb Vilnius group is working on this. In progress; no numbers.

Do not say: the Clebsch–Gordan weights (2/3, 1/3) for the two charge channels — textbook isospin, not in the cited papers; say "isospin partner".

## hexaquarks: Six quarks

**Message.** One six-quark bound state is established, the deuteron; heavy dibaryons are lattice predictions and the d*(2380) is a contested claim; at LHCb the reachable tests are baryon-number-zero spectra and, speculatively, charm-baryon correlations.

**On screen.**

One six-quark bound state is established: the deuteron, B = 2.22 MeV.

Everything else is a prediction (lattice QCD: ΩcΩcc, ΩbΩbb, ΩccbΩcbb) or a contested claim (d*(2380)).

PDG 2024; Junnarkar & Mathur, PRL 123 (2019) 162003; WASA-at-COSY, PRL 106 (2011) 242302

**Notes.**

Budget 1.25 min.

0:00–0:15  The one we know. Deuteron binding from the PDG 2024 constants table (CODATA 2018): m_p + m_n − m_d = 938.272 088 + 939.565 421 − 1875.612 943 = 2.224 566 MeV; say 2.22 MeV. PDG has no particle listing for the deuteron, and no dibaryon or hexaquark section at all in the 2024 Particle Listings. Terminology: dibaryon = two baryons, baryon number 2; baryonium = baryon–antibaryon, baryon number 0 (Wang & Wang, arXiv:2110.14133). ΛcΛ̄c and ΣcΣ̄c candidates are baryonium, not dibaryons.

0:15–0:35  Claimed: d*(2380). WASA-at-COSY, pn → dπ⁰π⁰: the ABC enhancement comes with a Lorentzian energy dependence of the cross section, consistent with a resonance I(J^P) = 0(3⁺) in the pn and ΔΔ systems (PRL 106 (2011) 242302 — title ends with a question mark). Polarised np scattering: A_y deviates from SAID SP07; adding the data produces a pole in the ³D₃–³G₃ waves at (2380 ± 10 − i 40 ± 5) MeV (PRL 112 (2014) 202301; PRC 90 (2014) 035204); M ≈ 2380 MeV, Γ ≈ 70 MeV. Status: one experimental programme (WASA at CELSIUS then COSY; A2@MAMI reports a photoproduction structure "consistent with" it, same lead authors); not in PDG; interpretation contested — Molina, Ikeno, Oset reproduce the np → π⁺π⁻d peak with sequential single-pion production and a triangle singularity (Chin. Phys. C 47 (2023) 041001), Bashkanov, Clement, Skorodko reply that the non-fusion channels and the pole cannot be made that way (arXiv:2106.00494), exchange ongoing. Lattice support at heavy pion masses: HAL QCD finds a ΔΔ ⁷S₃ quasi-bound state 25–40 MeV below threshold at m_π = 679–1018 MeV (PLB 811 (2020) 135935) — the physical d* is about 80 MeV below ΔΔ, so qualitative support only.

0:35–0:55  Predicted: heavier, more bound. Lattice QCD (Junnarkar & Mathur, PRL 123 (2019) 162003): J^P = 1⁺ dibaryons ΩcΩcc (sscscc, M = 6381(20) MeV, −26(9) MeV from the spin-1/2 threshold), ΩbΩbb (16004(17) MeV) and ΩccbΩcbb (19105(21) MeV) below their two-baryon thresholds, so stable against strong and electromagnetic decay; for the bottom systems the closer spin-3/2 threshold leaves only −29(13) and −52(13) MeV. Binding grows with quark mass. ΩcccΩccc (Lyu et al., PRL 127 (2021) 072003, HAL QCD near physical point): bound by 5.68 MeV without Coulomb, but with the ++/++ Coulomb repulsion a₀ = −19(7) fm — near unitarity, not clearly bound. Molecular models: ΞccΞcc loosely bound in one-boson exchange, binding 0.56 MeV at cutoff 1.2 GeV to 17.8 MeV at 1.5 GeV (Meng, Li, Zhu, PRD 95 (2017) 114019); heavy baryonia — models disagree.

0:55–1:20  What LHCb can touch. Marked speculation, mine, not from any paper: (i) a charmed dibaryon needs two charm baryons in one event; LHCb measures σ(Ξcc⁺⁺)·B/σ(Λc⁺) = (2.22 ± 0.27 ± 0.29)×10⁻⁴ at 13 TeV (Chin. Phys. C 44 (2020) 022001), so doubly charmed pairs are far out of reach — my estimate; (ii) the femtoscopy route ALICE used for p–Ω⁻ and p–Ξ⁻ in pp at 13 TeV (Nature 588 (2020) 232), where HAL QCD predicts an NΩ bound state at ≈2.5 MeV and ALICE sees no corresponding depletion, could in principle be applied to Λc–p or Λc–Λc pairs at LHCb; not done. Established, baryon number 0: B → ΛcΛ̄c(K) spectra. LHCb, 9 fb⁻¹: first observation of B̄s⁰ → Λc⁺Λ̄c⁻ (6.2σ, B = (5.0 ± 1.3 ± 0.5 ± 0.8)×10⁻⁵, W-exchange) and evidence for B̄⁰ → Λc⁺Λ̄c⁻ (4.3σ) (PRL 136 (2026) 061802) — two-body, so m(ΛcΛ̄c) is fixed at the B mass; not a threshold probe. B⁰ → Λc⁺Λ̄c⁻K⁰S, 5.4 fb⁻¹ (PRD 114 (2026) 012012): "No significant structure is seen in the Λc⁺Λc⁻ spectrum". Belle's near-threshold enhancement in e⁺e⁻ → Λc⁺Λc⁻ (M = 4634 +8/−7 +5/−8 MeV, Γ = 92 +40/−24 +10/−21 MeV, PRL 101 (2008) 172001) is not seen by BESIII at twelve energies 4.61–4.95 GeV (PRL 131 (2023) 191901); PDG 2024 files it under ψ(4660) with the footnote that the π⁺π⁻ψ(2S) and Λc⁺Λc⁻ states "are not necessarily the same". Kinematics: the Cabibbo-favoured b → cc̄s channels Λb⁰ → Λc⁺Λ̄c⁻Λ (threshold 5688.6 MeV vs m(Λb⁰) = 5619.6) are closed; so charmed baryon–antibaryon pairs at LHCb come from B-meson decays.

1:20–1:25  Close: sixty years after (q q q q q̄), the six-quark list is still the deuteron plus predictions.

## dropped (unverified)

- "Why these neutrals are hard at LHCb" synthesis (verdict real=false): the 40–95 MeV/c momentum range (omits the 137 MeV/c D̄*⁰ photon and 179 MeV/c Σc(2520)⁺ pion), the "25–30 % ECAL resolution at 0.1–0.2 GeV" extrapolation (not a measurement, wrong regime), and the lead converter as a loss mechanism. Only the individually verified facts (p* values, photon pT cuts, 8 MeV/c² π⁰ resolution) are used.
- Clebsch–Gordan weights 2/3 vs 1/3 for Σc⁺⁺D⁻ vs Σc⁺D̄⁰ in an I=1/2 Pc⁺ — textbook isospin, not in any cited paper; slide says "isospin partner" only.
- "~19 MeV below" wording for Pc(4440)⁺ — ambiguous (Pc(4457)−Pc(4440) = 17.0 MeV; 19.2 MeV is the gap to Σc⁺D̄*⁰); notes state which gap is meant.
- arXiv:1506.08614 (R(D*)) as a source for the two-fold ambiguity — misattribution; that paper uses (p_B)_z = (m_B/m_reco)(p_reco)_z with no quadratic. Two-fold ambiguity cited to arXiv:1504.01568 and arXiv:1709.01920 instead.
- Journal reference "Nature Physics 10 (2015) 1038" for arXiv:1504.01568 — arXiv metadata error; correct is Nature Physics 11 (2015) 743 (Crossref, INSPIRE).
- "Typical LHCb requirements" photon pT > 250 MeV/c — one analysis' selection (Govorkova, LHCb-PROC-2015-009), stated as such; the performance paper uses 200 MeV/c.
- Any statement that no Σc⁺D̄⁽*⁾⁰K⁻ search or missing-π⁰/γ recovery has ever been published — a negative that could not be proved; notes say only that arXiv:2404.19510 does not reconstruct it.
- LHCb Vilnius group results — described as in progress, no numbers, per instruction.
