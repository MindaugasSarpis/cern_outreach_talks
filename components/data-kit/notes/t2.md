## API (exactly as implemented)

**dk-spark.** FIELDS `pos name grains` (221 = 1 core + 220 ring grains). Options:
`steps`, `size` (2.5), `alpha` (1), `halo` `{r: 0.028, n: 220, size: 0.95}` (r is a
share of the frame's height), `haloAlpha` (1.35), `color` (`'sel'` | `'crowd'`), `seed`.
A step is `null` (hidden) or `{ at, from, glide, on, row, color, core, halo }`:
- `at`, with `from` and `glide`, as in MasterTalk;
- `on` is a form name or a list; the spark follows `api.ours()`, then
  `api.posOf(row)`, then `api.pointOf(row)` of the first form that answers;
- `core` defaults to off while following.

The api is `arm`, `assemble` (calls onDone at once), `busy` (glide, fade, armed),
`value()` 0, `ours()`, `ready()`. There are no event uniforms: the glide and glow are
computed from t0 on the world clock.

**dk-vertex.** FIELDS `pos name grains` (475 = 1 + 34 + 2×220). `files(o)` →
`['d0.json']` unless `m` and `pt` are given. Options: `at`, `heap`, `ours`, `dir`,
`flight`, `track`, `bend`, `land {of, m}`, `names`, `tracks[{name}]`, `namePx`, `seed`,
`face`, `flightTime`, `landTime`, `trail`, `strand`, and `m`/`pt`/`tau` overrides.
Steps: 0 hidden · 1 flight · 2 decay · 3 land. The whole story is a function of τ, its
own time from 0 to 6.2 s. A step forward runs τ from where it stands to the step's end.
An arrival or `c` runs τ from 0. A step back sets τ to the step's end. The api is
`arm`, `assemble` (onDone at once), `busy` (τ running, fading, hand-over, waiting for the
land frame), `value()` (row 1's M once folded, else 0), `values()`
`{m, pt, tau, pstar, pK, pPi, open, mass, flightMm, step}`, `ours()`, `ready()`.
Exported for tests: `decay({m, p, seed})`, `M_K`, `M_PI`.

**dk-bell.** FIELDS `pos name grains` (300 000). Options: `n` (300 000), `sigmas` 6,
`width` 24, `height` 8, `ticks`, `tickPx`, `word`, `seed` 401, `fill` 1.6, `alphaGain`
1.5, `selGain` 1.7. Steps merge like dk-hist's, through `stateAt`, imported from hist.js:
`{}`, `{lit: k}`, `{zoom: true | {from, gain}}`. The api is `arm`, `assemble` (onDone at
once), `busy` (fill, front sweep, zoom), `value()` (count beyond the moving front, from
all n draws), `values()` `{n, drawn, lit, count, max, gain, zoom}`, `ready()`.
Exported: `probit`, `bellDraws(n, seed)`, `beyond(x, k)`.

Every colour comes from `roles(ctx.palette)` (crowd, sel, name, tick, axis). Every
placement is seeded with mulberry32. Every motion runs on the world clock t. Each form
returns `frameScale` (its material's uPixelRatio), registers under its name and
unregisters, disposes what it made, and draws at most `ctx.budget` grains.

## Decisions (each with its alternative)

1. **The halo is a screen-space ring** with a fixed share of the frame (2.8 % of its
   height), facing the camera. The alternative, a world-space ring, shrinks to nothing
   at 25 units and goes elliptical under pitch.
2. **A followed spark draws only the ring.** The followed form already draws our grain
   at 2.5 in the slide's colour, so a second core would double its light. `core: true`
   overrides.
3. **The decay plane faces the camera.** cosθ* is simulated with a seed; the azimuth
   about the flight line is a choice of view. Seed 401's own azimuth (62°) threw the
   tracks nearly along the line of sight, and they read as one strand. The alternative
   is `face: false`.
4. **The bell's height is uniform under the curve** (area density constant), so it reads
   as a very fine histogram, and each grain's alpha follows how deeply the grains
   overlap. The alternative was stacked columns of 0.1 σ bins, which make the same filled
   shape with steps.
5. **Stratified draws** (one jittered u per 1/n stratum) rather than i.i.d. u. They are
   still seeded inverse-CDF sampling, and the counts match n·(1 − Φ) to a grain, so the
   numbers on screen are the expected ones. With i.i.d. draws, 405 would wobble by ±20,
   and a grain beyond 5 σ would turn up in about 8 % of seeds.
6. **The zoom stretches the tail by 0.6·e^(from²/2)**, not by e^(from²/2). At full
   height the tail became a thin wall at 3 σ.
7. **The re-roll stops after 12 rolls** (14.4 s), so the slide settles for shots and the
   recorder. The alternative, an endless loop, never settles (seen).
