# t1: dk-glow, dk-trigger, dk-lineup, dk-ground (29 Oct 2026)

Talk 1's forms, also used in T4 (the trigger gate and its collapse) and T5 (lineup, the
crane back). The stubs `glow.js`, `trigger.js`, `lineup.js` and `ground.js` are replaced.
The README has a section for each type (fields, steps, api, an example object).

## API in one place

| type | required | steps | api |
|---|---|---|---|
| dk-glow | pos name grains (+ `of`, `src`) | 0 off · 1 fronts at f0 · 2 the fronts merge into a glow | value() fronts sent; busy while merging or fading; ready() |
| dk-trigger | pos name grains | 0 hidden · 1 runs · 1+k gates 1..k on · `collapse` (default gates+2) | value() Π keep·shrink of the active gates; ours() our packet's world position; busy while showing, while ours glides, during a burst wave, while collapsing |
| dk-lineup | pos name grains balls | k shows piles 0..k-1; an array shows exactly those | value() TB the shown piles stand for; arm/assemble (scatter on a flight, gather on arrival); busy while gathering |
| dk-ground | pos grains | none (always on); with `name`: 0 hidden, ≥ 1 shown | value() grains |

Every module registers itself under its name (`kit.register`), so another form can find
it with `kit.form(name)`.

## Lifts and what changed

- **dk-trigger** comes from MasterTalk's `setup/route.js:32-177` `stream`.
  - Gates: 2 → 4 (`MAXG`), each `{ x, keep, shrink, ring }`.
  - MasterTalk's unsourced 0.06 keep is gone, and no gate has a default keep.
  - New: `shrink` (Turbo: a kept packet loses most of its grains), empty crossings (fainter, a few grains, dropped at every gate), a buffer that holds packets (parked knots on a turning spheroid shell; packets move `hold` times slower in its span and ride out onto it), a fire flash on the gate ring, the collapse into a ball, and `ours` as an x, an [x, y, z] or `{ at, fold }`.
  - New: the burst wave. When a gate fires, the packets it drops that are already past it burst on a wave from the gate (30 units/s), so the lane past it empties at once. In MasterTalk only the packets crossing after the firing burst, and the lane emptied over about 12 s.
  - Gate on/off times go through `eventLatch`. Each fade lasts until the last packet the gate burst has run off the lane, so a reset never makes a dropped packet reappear mid-lane.
- **dk-glow** ports Užsikrauk karjerai's `setup/detector.js` `cascade` as an overlay on the kit's lhcb detector, because the engine's detector shader cannot take KJ's uniforms.
  - It fetches the same geometry file (`stage-data/lhcb-grains.bin`), takes every k-th grain, uses the same frame and KJ's part weights, and follows the detector's transform through `of`.
  - The phase is f0 up to step 2, then f0·e^(r t) (continuous), merging between 1 and 2.2 Hz as in KJ.
  - Its times (`uT0`, `uHideT`, `uT2`) go through `eventLatch`.
- **dk-lineup** is Užsikrauk karjerai's `setup/piles.js:16-496`, with the shaders kept.
  - The key light is neutral white (KJ's was warm), and colours are palette roles.
  - Seeded: lattice shell order, jitter, seeds.
  - Piles rest on the floor by default. Labels are kit fixed-size labels.
  - A budget thinning keeps the volume (coarser spheres).
  - Steps are 1-based (`k` shows k piles). KJ's 0 showed the first pile.
- **dk-ground** is Užsikrauk karjerai's `setup/piles.js:497-552` `floor`, on GRAIN_GLSL (obeys `steady`). It is dim, not light blue. The default alpha is 1.0, because the dim colour through the fragment's (c·a)^2.2 is very dark: 0.32 was invisible.

### What the review fix changed

- **dk-glow colour grammar** (`glow.js`): a front is every crossing, so it stays crowd light
  blue. The colour mix is now `mix(crowd, sel, clamp(0.25·pulse + 0.1·merged + spark))`, and
  the pulse shows through alpha (`1.4·pulse + 0.4·merged + 0.9·spark`). Only the sparse
  sparkle of hits goes white. Seen on slide 2: the slab is light blue. Only the VELO core at
  the beam saturates toward white under the bloom, where hundreds of grains overlap.
- **Detector in the palette** (lab `space.json`; the recommended object is in `glow.js`'s
  header): `parts: { preset: lhcb, velo: { alpha: 0.25 }, pipe: { alpha: 0 }, magnet: { color:
  '#7dd3fc', alpha: 0.25 }, rich1/rich2/hcal: { color: '#7dd3fc' } }`. The royal and navy
  blues of the engine's PART_COLORS are gone from slides 1-3. A kit preset computed from the
  palette would need a core change; see requests-t1.md item 5.
- **dk-trigger** (`trigger.js`):
  - The buffer is now parked packets: knots of `per` grains on a prolate-spheroid shell round
    the lane (a Fibonacci sphere stretched to `len` × `r`), turning as one at `spin` rad/s. It
    is never a filled disc. Packets in its span ride out onto the shell and back, but only
    while the buffer is on (`uBufOn`). Before this, kept packets past gate 1 rode out at step
    2 too, scattering above and below the lane on slide 4.
  - `knot` (0.1): the packet's spread. The lab uses 0.08 with lane 0.7, so a packet is a knot.
  - Phases are stratified (one packet a slot, jittered), so the dense lane is a steady stream,
    not Poisson clumps. The keeps stay random.
  - `minPx` (0, off): a floor on the grain size in px, for a wide shot. The lab does not need it.
  - busy also covers the buffer's fade.

### Judgement (looked at, honestly)

- **Glow merged (3): good**, as before, now without the saturated blues. **Glow fronts (2):
  fair to good.** The slab is light blue, and the motion still carries it.
- **Gate 1 (4): good.** A dense light-blue river runs into the gate. Past it, our white packet
  and a few knots spread over the same width of lane. Square to the lane, equal lengths take
  equal widths, so the eye compares a full stream against about 5 knots. That is not a
  countable 30:1, but it does read as "almost everything stops here". The numbers are two
  .nums lines (30 MHz, 1 MHz) with no arrow glyph.
- **Buffer (5): good.** A cocoon of distinct knots round the lane, with the lane entering and
  leaving at its tips and gate 2's ring visible at the right. It reads as "packets parked in a
  store", not a solid blob. One risk: at a glance it could read as "a ball of something"
  without the narration, so the notes should say "the kept crossings wait here".
- **Gate 2 (6): fair to good.** Whole knots of 14 grains come in from the left, the gate ring
  stands in the middle with a little burst dust past it, and on the right only single grains go
  on, with our white packet (core plus one grain) among them. "Knots in, specks out" reads. The
  frame is still mostly black above and below the lane. At the honest 1/30 flux, only 2 or 3
  packets sit on each side of the gate. Our shrunk packet reads as a white star (the OURS core
  under bloom), not as a shrunk knot.
- **Lineup (8, 9): good.** `4 TB/s` now fades in at 1 s, while the single sphere is in frame.
  On slide 9 the 1- and 4-piles sit clear of the numbers. The big piles keep their halo (low:
  not changed; it is KJ's metal look, which the owner chose).

## Open

1. **Gate 2 frame density.** The honest flux after gate 1 leaves 2-3 packets each side of gate
   2 in a close shot. If the owner wants a fuller gate-2 picture, the honest options are a
   still closer pose, or a sourced HLT2 keep share with gate 2 dropping packets too (both
   hold the ratio). The trigger budget is not the limit.
2. **Gate 2's numbers.** The lab uses `keep: 1, shrink: 0.075` (30/400: the ×400 overall byte
   ratio after HLT1's ×30). That is a computed split, not a sourced HLT2 keep share, so it
   draws no packet drops at gate 2. The bible's "drops packets" needs a sourced
   `lhcb-hlt2-output-rate` (a gap in the bible).
3. **The bible says "the buffer disc".** The kit now draws a shell of parked packets (the
   review's fix). The Talk 1 author should write "buffer" in the notes, not "disc".
4. **The glow's rate is stylised** (0.6 fronts a second). The notes must say that the real
   25 ns cannot be drawn and that the fronts stand for crossings.
5. **dk-glow requires the lhcb plugin and a detector naming the same `src`.** Without them it
   warns once and draws nothing. check.mjs does not check this yet (see requests-t1.md).
6. **Crossing.vue / slideNav.js** (bible §4) are not in the kit. The lab's Crossing.vue is a
   stand-in.
7. **dk-ground's** `"enterable": true` must be on the object for stage:check.
8. **README** (core's file): the dk-trigger and dk-glow sections now lag the code (buffer
   shell, `knot`, `minPx`, spin; glow colours). The text to paste is in requests-t1.md item 6.
