lhcb-grains.bin: points sampled from the surfaces of LHCb's Run 3 detector geometry
(hero/bake_grains.py in this talk; Int16 [x, y, z, part] in mm, LHCb's frame;
Innoday's copy re-baked on 20 Oct 2026 with 4.8 times the grains, 446 400).

Source geometry: LHCb's DD4hep detector description, https://gitlab.cern.ch/lhcb/Detector
