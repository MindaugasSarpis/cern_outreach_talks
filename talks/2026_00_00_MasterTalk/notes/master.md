# MasterTalk as the master deck

Proposal, 26 Oct 2026 (iteration 1). The owner's brief, through the Scheduler:
"inheriting from all previous talks, introducing CERN, LHCb and everything".
The master deck holds the best version of each element from the earlier
talks; venue talks are cut from it. This file is the plan. The deck's
Status and Decisions are in `../CLAUDE.md`.

## Does "one collision, one shot" carry it all?

Partly. The approved spine follows one real LHCb collision from the beam
pipe, through the detector and the trigger, to the one dot it becomes in the
2019 peak. That carries the machine, LHCb, the data and the pentaquarks well:
each of them is a station the collision passes. Four required elements are
not on the collision's path:

- **The zoom** from Vilnius to the cosmic web and the funnel (Innoday,
  Karjera): a prologue before the collision exists.
- **CERN and the LHC** as the earlier talks show them: three real clips
  (CERN from above, the tunnel, the dipole) and `lhcb.mp4` with LHCb's
  people. Clips are cuts, so a strict "one shot" cannot hold them.
- **The inventions** (Innoday): nine things that left CERN. None of them is
  on the collision's path.
- **The close** (Innoday, Karjera): the flag, then the recorded flight out to
  the LHC and the FCC. It is a recording, so it is also a cut.

Three ways to hold them. **This is the owner's call; iteration 1 built only
what holds under all three** (the prologue and the way in, below).

## Venue cuts

Each venue talk is a copy of the master with slides dropped, in this order:

- **~25 min, business audience (Innoday-like):** drop 22, 25, 20–21; merge
  14 and 15; keep every invention strand.
- **~15 min, school or TV (Karjera-like):** as for 25 min, and drop the
  readout and trigger stations (13–15) and the strands except the web; 23–24
  become one slide.
- **~7 min, award (OpenData-like):** 1, 6–7, 10, 12, 16, 18, 19, 24, 28.

## Rules every inherited piece keeps (Karjera, filmed 26 Oct)

- **A heavy finale is a recording**, muted, with Soundtrack's `drop`
  slaving the clip to the music. Karjera's live finale (~1.1 M grains) went
  white on the owner's M2.
- **Under ~600 000 grains a slide.** The web slide here is 520 000 (Karjera
  ran 740 000).
- **No accent on the world clock.** Karjera's music accent missed because the
  world's clock and the audio clock drift apart. An accent is timed off the
  audio (Soundtrack) or the clip.
- **`detector.js`'s `uCrossT`/`uCascT` are reset on stop** when Karjera's
  detector is copied (its point cloud blinked from the start).
- English; no new speaker scripts (each inherited slide keeps its message,
  picture and sources, but not the Lithuanian script); minimal on-screen
  text; no advance-on-end; photoreal.
