lhcb-grains.bin: points sampled from the surfaces of LHCb's Run 3 detector geometry
(hero/bake_grains.py in this talk; Int16 [x, y, z, part] in mm, LHCb's frame;
Innoday's copy re-baked on 20 Oct 2026 with 4.8 times the grains, 446 400; again the same
day at 3.8 times on the faces and 0.3 more along the feature edges, 459 420, with
Blender 4.5.14 LTS).
lhcb-grains.shape.bin: each grain's face normal and edge flag (the same bake).
lhcb-mesh.glb: the same geometry as decimated surfaces, one object a sub-detector
(hero/export_mesh.py).

Source geometry: LHCb's DD4hep detector description, https://gitlab.cern.ch/lhcb/Detector
