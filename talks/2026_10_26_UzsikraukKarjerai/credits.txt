Data
- Slides 9–10: LHCb collaboration, Phys. Rev. Lett. 118, 182001 (2017), arXiv:1703.04639, CC BY 4.0: the m(Ξc⁺K⁻) of Fig. 2 (3.3 fb⁻¹), its 1 MeV bin contents read from the vector figure (LHCb-PAPER-2017-002/Fig2.pdf) and summed to 2 MeV (they equal the paper's Fig. 3 bins), 2960–3300 MeV, 59 453 candidates, drawn one grain per two candidates.
- LHCb collaboration, Phys. Rev. Lett. 122, 222001 (2019): the m(J/ψ p) bins drawn on slides 11 and 12, from HEPData ins1728691, Table 2 (doi:10.17182/hepdata.89271.v1/t2).
- Slide 8 and the left plot of slide 12: an illustration generated for this talk (no measured data).
- Slide 6, the collision: one real CMS crossing from CERN Open Data record 300 (CC0), run 195099, event 137440354 (27 May 2012, 8 TeV, a Higgs boson candidate); its provenance is in components/lhc-opening/README.md.
- Slide 18, the sun of plots: made by LHCb Vilnius for OpenData from two public LHCb files, CERN Open Data records 401 and 24506 (public/figures/opendata/, copied from talks/2026_10_21_OpenData).
- Slides 15–17, the piles: drawn for OpenData; the volumes are in each slide's notes.
