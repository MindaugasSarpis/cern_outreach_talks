# Tokyo, 15 min: blueprint (5 Nov 2026, for the owner's approval)

## Why

The owner's review of the 5 Nov deploy: the storytelling is off and the flow is not solid; LHCb
was presented by its weight; the quarks need careful steps; Sakata shows in passing but is
skipped; the NFTMC zoom and the space shot were out of place but are good shots; real video of
the LHC and LHCb, as Innoday and Užsikrauk karjerai (the Delfi talk) had it; more words on the
slides: no titles, but captions. And the finale: LHCb's collision with the low bang, in to a
pentaquark being made, out to Tokyo.

Room: 15:15–15:30, no Q&A; diplomats, RIKEN researchers, Japanese and Lithuanian guests, many
listening in a second language. Yamaguchi (Nagoya) speaks next, on the theory. Projector
1280 × 800; the room's audio may be poor (no music; the finale's low bang only).

## One line

Everything around us is made of a few pieces. Japanese physicists named them step by step;
LHCb, with Vilnius in it, found five of them bound together; Vilnius and Nagoya now find out
how. The talk comes down from space to CERN at the start and back down to Tokyo at the end.

## Style (every slide)

- No titles. Each slide has one caption: what is shown and the one thing to take from it,
  a sentence or a phrase, white on a shade, bottom left, the projector floor (20 px canvas)
  or larger. A number keeps its big type, top corner, with its word.
- Clips are real footage (the library and Karjera's) or our own recorded scenes; they arrive
  and leave as dust. A clip's caption fades in after 1 s.
- Spoken greeting in Japanese and Lithuanian on the cover; nothing on screen for it.

## The finale (one recorded clip, ~35 s, with its sound)

1. LHCb's point cloud at eye level (Užsikrauk karjerai's close): the collision crosses, a low
   swell, a thump, the drone (the shared opening's synth, rendered onto the clip).
2. The camera pushes into the flash; Karjera's cover pentaquark (u u d c c̄, gluon springs)
   gathers out of a second crossing, a softer thump.
3. Out: the pentaquark shrinks to a point among the stars; the space shot (cosmic web back to
   the Earth's limb, vilnius_back) brings us home; the limb breaks into grains onto the globe,
   where Tokyo is lit.
4. Held on the globe: "Thank you · ありがとうございました · Ačiū".
